# Code of Conduct

OpenReason exists so that disagreement can be about reasoning, not about people. The same applies to how we work together.

## Our standard

We expect everyone taking part in the project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any project channel, to:

- Argue with evidence and sources, and accept that being shown better reasoning is the point, not a loss.
- Be direct and be kind. Critique the document, the schema, the code; not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and people from every background, discipline and jurisdiction. Neutrality is a design goal of the protocol and a norm of its community.
- Respect that maintainers may decline a change on principle (see CONTRIBUTING) without that being personal.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **conduct@publicreasonproject.org**. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers. Outcomes range from a private warning to removal from project spaces, proportionate to the behaviour.

This document adapts the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, in shorter form.
