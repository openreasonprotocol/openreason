<section className="pr-sec">
<div className="pr-tag">Fork and re-run <b>02</b></div>
<p className="pr-body">Because assumptions are explicit, anyone can <strong>fork</strong> a document, change one
assumption, and <strong>re-run</strong> it. Disagreement becomes <em>locatable</em> — you can point to the
exact assumption or source where two parties diverge, instead of talking past each other.</p>
</section>

<section className="pr-sec" style={{ borderBottom: 'none' }}>
<div className="pr-tag">Verify · v0.3 <b>03</b></div>
<p className="pr-body">A document can carry a cryptographic <strong>attestation</strong> — a detached Ed25519
signature over its canonical form. A reader (a person, or an agent on a rail) verifies it <strong>offline</strong>:
recompute the hash, check the signature against a key it resolves itself, confirm the key hasn't expired. No
need to trust, or even contact, the publisher.</p>
<p className="pr-body" style={{ marginTop: '14px' }}>Verification yields a <strong>trust tier</strong> —
<code>self_declared → attested → verified → full</code> — and a claimed tier never exceeds what the signatures
prove. For data products, a <strong>usage policy</strong> then says what may be done, which a rail enforces at
activation.</p>
