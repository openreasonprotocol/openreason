<p className="pr-body">
An ORP document is reasoning you can re-run — five structured layers, from where the data came from to how
the conclusion was reached, that anyone can fork and re-run with different assumptions. Disagreement becomes
<em>locatable</em>: you can point to the exact assumption two parties diverge on.
</p>
<p className="pr-body" style={{ marginTop: '14px' }}>

offline and know exactly how far to trust it.
