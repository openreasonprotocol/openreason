<div className="pr-eyebrow">OpenReason · What it is</div>
<h1 className="pr-h1">Reasoning you can inspect, fork, and <span className="accent">verify</span>.</h1>
<p className="pr-lead">OpenReason Protocol records <em>how</em> a decision was reached — where the data came from, what was
assumed, who is affected, who decided what — in a structured, machine-readable form. Not a report you take on
trust. A <strong>simulatable object</strong>: anyone can fork it and re-run it with different assumptions to
see what changes.</p>

<div className="pr-tag">Two readers, one object <b>01</b></div>
<div className="pr-two">
  <div>
    <div className="pr-k">For people</div>
    <h3>Inspect &amp; challenge</h3>
    <p>Document, inspect, fork and challenge the reasoning behind a decision. Trust comes from argument and
    cited sources — not authority.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <div className="pr-k">For machines</div>
    <h3>Verify &amp; enforce</h3>
    <p>Verify a dataset offline, enforce what may be done with it, and prove use — a signed passport an agent
    checks itself. Added in v0.3.</p>
  </div>
</div>
</section>

<section className="pr-sec">
<div className="pr-tag">Who it's for <b>02</b></div>
<div className="pr-grid">
  <div className="pr-cell"><div className="t">Policymakers &amp; analysts</div><div className="d">Publish reasoning others can scrutinise on substance, not politics.</div></div>
  <div className="pr-cell"><div className="t">Researchers &amp; AI teams</div><div className="d">Document dataset provenance, consent and limitations under a real standard.</div></div>
  <div className="pr-cell"><div className="t">Agents &amp; rails</div><div className="d">Accept a data product only when its passport verifies and its usage policy allows.</div></div>
</div>
</section>

<section className="pr-sec" style={{ borderBottom: 'none' }}>
<div className="pr-tag">What it is not <b>03</b></div>
<p className="pr-body">OpenReason exposes reasoning; it does not recommend conclusions, fact-check claims, or
resolve disagreement by vote. It attests to provenance and reasoning — <strong>never to the truth of the
data</strong>.</p>
