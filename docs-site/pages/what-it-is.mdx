# What it is

The **Public Reason Project** builds open infrastructure for one idea: the reasoning behind a consequential
decision should be inspectable, challengeable, and — now — verifiable. **OpenReason Protocol (ORP)** is the
standard that makes that possible.

An ORP document records *how* a decision was reached — where the data came from, what was assumed, who is
affected, who decided what — in a structured, machine-readable form. It is not a report you take on trust.
It is a **simulatable object**: anyone can fork it and re-run it with different assumptions to see what
changes.

## Who it's for

- **Policymakers & analysts** — publish reasoning others can scrutinise on substance, not politics.
- **Researchers & AI teams** — document dataset provenance, consent and limitations under a real standard.
- **Agents & rails** — accept a data product only when its passport verifies and its usage policy allows.

## What it is *not*

OpenReason exposes reasoning; it does not recommend conclusions, fact-check claims, or resolve disagreement
by vote. It attests to provenance and reasoning — never to the truth of the data.
